# Higgsfield — خلفية غرفة وكلاء عقار أوب

## Prompt

Animate this hand-drawn systems-map keyframe as a subtle seamless website background loop.
Preserve the warm architectural tracing-paper texture, graphite-and-ink style, muted ochre,
faded teal, and clay watercolor accents. The camera makes an extremely slow 2.5D push-in
with barely perceptible left-to-right parallax, as if the viewer is leaning over a living
executive sketchbook.

Pencil connector paths gently draw themselves in short segments, pause, then softly fade
back to their original state. Small paper notes orbit very slowly along imperfect hand-drawn
ellipses, with natural paper tilt of no more than 1–2 degrees. A few graphite particles and
eraser crumbs drift subtly across the paper. Watercolor washes breathe almost imperceptibly.
One selection circle redraws itself twice with a human pencil stroke, then rests. Ink arrows
pulse by being retraced—not by glowing. Paper fibers respond to soft ambient light.

Create a calm 8-second seamless loop. The first and last frames must match. Motion must remain
slow enough for Arabic interface text placed above the video to stay perfectly readable.
Keep the center and right side composition stable for interactive UI overlays.

## Negative Prompt

No new text, no changing letters, no morphing portraits, no face movement, no talking,
no blinking, no camera shake, no fast zoom, no dramatic rotation, no floating camera,
no neon, no glow, no holograms, no 3D metallic objects, no photorealistic room, no page turn,
no new cards, no disappearing objects, no warped paper, no flicker, no heavy dust,
no depth-of-field blur over the main systems, no logo, no watermark.

## إعدادات مقترحة

- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Duration: 8 seconds
- Loop: seamless
- Motion strength: low (15–25%)
- Camera: slow push-in + micro parallax
- Stylization: preserve source / high image adherence
- Output: 1920×1080 MP4, H.264, muted/no audio
